About Me

I'm studying Information Technology, and most of my time goes into web development and backend projects. Outside of coding, I'm drawn to philosophy, especially social philosophy, because I enjoy asking why people and communities behave the way they do.

I also enjoy reading, writing, music and acting. For the past five years, I've volunteered in my community: three years teaching moral education to younger students and helping with school drives, followed by two years with the National Service Scheme (NSS).

Through NSS, I've taken part in community drives and attended two camps. These experiences have strengthened my sense of responsibility, teamwork and connection to the community.
